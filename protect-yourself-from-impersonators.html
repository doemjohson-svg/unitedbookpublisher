Beware of Imitators

Security Alert

These Are Scams. Not United Book Publisher.

Imitation may be flattering. Impersonation is not. Our success has attracted websites and entities attempting to replicate our online presence, including our name, branding, design, content, and website structure. These unauthorized imitations may create confusion and cause authors to mistakenly believe they are interacting with United Book Publisher.

We built this page to give authors a reliable way to identify the official United Book Publisher and recognize websites that are not authorized to represent us. If you encounter a site claiming to be us, use the verification tool below before sharing information or engaging their services.

They are not us. They are not affiliated with us. And we are actively warning authors about them. If you encounter a suspected impersonator, please report it and share this page to help protect other authors.
